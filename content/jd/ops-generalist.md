# Operations Manager

Take a department that has no head and make it work.

## Who we are

We are a small trading firm in alternative assets. Around fifty people. We are
rebuilding the operating side from close to first principles this quarter. We
are hiring four operators.

## The job

Each of these four seats takes something that has no owner today. Depending on
what you are good at, that is quant support, technical support, client
operations, quality assurance, customer service, project management, or the
knowledge base. We decide which after we have talked, not before.

In every case the work is the same shape. Find out how the thing actually
works, write it down, measure the error rate, and then fix it.

## You

- You have built or fixed a process and can name the number that moved.
- Meticulous. You catch mistakes other people do not.
- You write one message that contains enough context to be answered in one
  round trip. This is a real daily constraint here, not a nice to have.
- Comfortable with buggy, badly built software and with ambiguity.
- Professional in correspondence, including holding a polite and persistent
  line when someone owes us something.

## What we are bad at, stated up front

- Almost none of our standard procedures exist.
- Nothing logs errors anywhere in the business.
- Several departments have no head at all.

If that reads as a warning, this is not your role.

## How the process runs

Written questions first, before any call. Then a timed simulation of the
actual work. We do this because our own hiring data says the interview is the
worst predictor we have, and prior work history is the best. You will be
judged on what you produce.
