# Head of Operations

Own how the company runs, so the founder can stop being the operating system.

## Who we are

We are a small trading firm in alternative assets. Around fifty people. We
have been profitable, we have been unprofitable, and we are rebuilding the
operating side from close to first principles this quarter. You would be one
of the people doing that rebuilding.

## The job

- Own the departments that have no head, and the processes that have no owner.
- Build and run the task system. Every request assigned, threaded, deadlined,
  status tracked, and closed only by the person who asked for it. Today things
  are raised in chat, forgotten, re-raised, and argued about on calls weeks
  later.
- Build the error log and the escalation path across three departments. It is
  the biggest cost line nobody measures. Single mistakes have cost tens of
  thousands.
- Write and enforce the missing standard procedures.
- Hold department leads to weekly scorecards.
- Specify what the internal systems need to do, and work with engineering to
  land it.

## You

- Comfortable with probabilistic thinking, and with problems where the
  objectives are in tension. The work is abstract more often than not.
- A person whose "I have got it" can be trusted. That is the most important
  line here.
- A player coach. You will do low leverage grunt work yourself to find out how
  something actually works before you systematise it.
- Fast. A systemiser. You use AI tools every day.
- Willing to hold people accountable, including people who have been here
  longer than you.

Not required: a finance background, or a specific degree. The domain is
explained on the call.

## What we are bad at, stated up front

- We have no financial reporting worth the name. The books tell you what was
  spent, not what to decide.
- Our error rates are unmeasured in three departments.
- Most of our systems run through one engineer.
- We have hired badly for this seat. Four attempts in two years. The last one
  lasted eleven months and was net negative at any salary.

If that reads as a warning, this is not your role. If it reads as a list you
could fix in six months, we should talk.

## What you get

- A named problem that is yours, and the authority to change how it works,
  rather than a queue of tickets.
- A founder who has already written down what he thinks is wrong. You are not
  reverse engineering the strategy from meetings.
- Fast decisions. There is no committee.
- Pay at the top of the range for the seat. The range is set by what the work
  is worth to us, not by a band.

## How the process runs

Written questions first, before any call. Then a timed simulation of the
actual work. Then an open ended one, where we measure what you choose to do
with unlimited time. We do this because our own hiring data says the interview
is the worst predictor we have, and prior work history is the best. You will
be judged on what you produce.

Intro call, then the timed sim, then a working session with the founder on a
real problem we have, then the open ended sim, then an offer.
