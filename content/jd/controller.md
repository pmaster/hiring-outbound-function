# Controller

Build the finance function. There has never been an accounting pass.

## Who we are

We are a small trading firm in alternative assets. Around fifty people. We have
real revenue, real cash movement, and no financial reporting worth the name.
The books tell you what was spent. They do not tell you what to decide.

## The job

You would be the first real finance hire, and you would build the function
rather than maintain one.

- The accounting pass that has never been done. Assets, runway, forward plan.
- The tax structure. It is advised by a specialist and proven elsewhere at over
  four thousand contractors.
- Loss measurement. Every number in the business today is stated before losses
  and unhaircut.
- Capital entity and intercompany agreements. Real service agreements, own bank
  accounts, own books, arm's length terms.
- Float and recall policy. Buffer sizes, recall cadence, per entity floats.
- Payment rails selection. Check each provider's acceptable use policy against
  what the business actually does, before committing.

## You

- You have built a finance function from nothing. What existed when you arrived
  and what existed when you left is the question we will ask.
- Multi entity and intercompany work is familiar, not new.
- Comfortable with treasury and cash management, not only with reporting.
- Contractor payments at volume is a plus.

Audit only, with no operating seat, is not the right fit here.

## What we are bad at, stated up front

- No accounting pass has ever been done.
- Payroll is run personally by the founder.
- All economics are stated pre loss.
- The single unsolved operational bottleneck is a money movement question, and
  it is waiting on a decision rather than a build.
