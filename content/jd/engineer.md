# Engineer

Own the internal tooling that fifty people work in every day.

## Who we are

We are a small trading firm in alternative assets. Around fifty people. We are
rebuilding the operating side from close to first principles this quarter.
Right now one engineer holds every internal system we have. We are hiring two
more, senior end first.

## The job

Architecture, scaffolding, DevOps and data engineering behind the internal
tooling the company runs on.

Stated honestly: no consumer platform, no extreme scale, no unusual uptime
demands. What is needed is good internal tooling and real data engineering.
The operations system and the CRM talking to each other. A money movement
coordination layer. Document audit tooling. Client quality capture. Dashboards
whose definitions people actually trust.

About twenty live initiatives sit on this seat and most of them have nobody on
them. You would be halving a single point of failure, not joining a backlog.

## You

- Not junior. General competence and operational judgment matter more here
  than deep specialism.
- Startup experience. You have shipped something from nothing.
- You use AI tools daily. That is expected, not a nice to have.
- You acknowledge a request the day it arrives, say what you will and will not
  do, and close the loop. This is written deliberately. The failure mode we
  are trying to avoid is silence, then denial, then a late partial fix.

Remote. Any region for the right person, though most of the team overlaps with
United States hours.

## What we are bad at, stated up front

- Nothing logs errors anywhere in the business.
- No cohort view of anything exists.
- Dashboards exist and nobody trusts the numbers on them. That was a
  definitions failure, not a visualisation failure.
- Almost none of our procedures are written down.
