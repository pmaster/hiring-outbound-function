# Head of Brand and Funnel

Own every conversion step from the ad to the offer.

## Who we are

We are a small trading firm in alternative assets. Around fifty people. We run
a high volume application funnel and we are rebuilding it this quarter, brand
included. Right now the founder does this work personally, which is why the
seat exists.

## The job

- The new brand and the funnel build. This is the largest single piece of work
  in flight.
- Funnel version two. The specification exists. The rebuild does not.
- A separate employer brand, with its own funnel, creatives and site.
- The rejection experience. Around thirty thousand applicants a month currently
  hear nothing. That is a real asset being thrown away every month.
- The reviews plan, and the video sales letter.
- Launch quality assurance across every brand surface.

## You

- You own conversion rate, not impressions. You can name a funnel you rebuilt
  and the before and after numbers.
- Direct response depth. Video sales letters, paid social, creative testing,
  cost per acquisition.
- You have launched a brand from nothing, including the positioning.
- Recruitment marketing or marketplace supply experience is a strong plus,
  because the funnel here is people, not products.

Brand work with no numbers attached is the wrong half of this seat.

## What we are bad at, stated up front

- Two of the biggest conversion levers in the business are unmeasured and
  unowned.
- Nobody does paid acquisition. The founder calls it the easy half and it still
  does not happen.
- The one number that decides both the media line and the volume requirement,
  application to booking, has never been baselined.
