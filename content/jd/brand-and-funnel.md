# Head of Brand and Funnel

Own every conversion step from the ad to the offer.

## Who we are

We are a small trading firm in alternative assets. Around fifty people. We
have been profitable, we have been unprofitable, and we are rebuilding the
operating side from close to first principles this quarter.

Our growth side is a funnel with about thirty thousand applications a month
entering it. Almost every stage of that funnel leaks, most of the leaks are
unmeasured, and none of them has an owner.

## Why this seat exists

Twelve open pieces of work sit on this seat. Seven of them are on the founder
today, which makes this the single largest block of his time that one hire
removes outright.

## The job

- **Build the new brand and the funnel behind it.** The largest piece of work
  in flight, and it is currently being done by the founder between everything
  else.
- **Rebuild the funnel.** The specification exists. Two stages leak most of
  the volume: the show rate on booked sessions sits between 45 and 55 percent
  and needs to reach 80, and completion after offer sits near 25 percent and
  needs to reach 50 or 60. Those two numbers compounded are roughly a two and
  a half times improvement in the whole business.
- **Build the employer brand.** A separate, deliberately boring one, with its
  own funnel, creatives and site. It does not exist.
- **Fix the rejection experience.** Thirty thousand applicants a month
  currently hear nothing at all. The specification is written. Nobody has
  built it.
- **Own the reviews problem.** We have a cold start and an old brand carrying
  accumulated damage.
- **Run the launch quality pass.** Several brands, and the presentation has to
  be right on each one independently.
- **Instrument application to booking.** This is the one number that decides
  both the media budget and the volume requirement. Today it sits at about 2.3
  percent and the plan needs 15.

## You

- You have owned a conversion funnel end to end and can name the number you
  moved and by how much.
- You have launched a brand, not just managed one.
- You are a direct response person before you are a brand person. Both is
  ideal. Brand without numbers is not a fit here.
- You have worked with high volume applicant or lead funnels, where the
  bottleneck is qualification rather than traffic.
- You write copy yourself, or you can direct it precisely enough that the
  writer does not have to guess.
- You are comfortable that several brands exist for reasons you will be told
  on the call, and that they stay operationally separate.

## What we are bad at, stated up front

- Two of the biggest conversion levers in the business are unmeasured and
  unowned.
- We have had recurring platform access problems and we do not know the root
  cause with certainty.
- The dashboards that exist are not trusted, because nobody agreed the
  definitions before someone built them.
- Nobody does paid acquisition today. The founder calls it the easy half and
  it still is not happening.

If that reads as a warning, this is not your role. If it reads as a list you
could fix in six months, we should talk.

- Full ownership of brand, funnel and acquisition. No committee.
- A founder who has already written the specifications and wants them
