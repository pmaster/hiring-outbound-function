# Chief of Staff

Take the low leverage work off the founder, and build the playbook where none
exists.

## Who we are

We are a small trading firm in alternative assets. Around fifty people. We are
rebuilding the operating side from close to first principles this quarter. The
founder is currently also the acting operations lead, the product owner and the
project manager. That is the problem this seat exists to fix.

## The job

- Take back the executive time that goes on work nobody else owns. The target
  is 75 percent of it.
- Fix the recruiting funnel. Interview no-show sits at 60 percent and document
  revision at 80 percent. Both are yours.
- Run time studies on remote specialists and set a quota that holds.
- Stand up customer support version one, then hand it to whoever runs it.
- Launch two or three new state or project initiatives a quarter and hand each
  one off.

Note what that list is. It is not a support role. Every item is a thing you own
until it works, then give away.

## You

- An adaptable generalist. You will move from a hiring problem to a hardware
  logistics problem to a vendor problem in one afternoon.
- A process architect. You audit a fractured workflow, map the version that
  works, and write requirements clear enough for someone else to build.
- A ruthless prioritiser. You will ignore three small fires to put out the one
  costing real money.
- Able to say no on the founder's behalf and make it stick.
- Autonomous. Given an objective, you build the playbook rather than asking for
  one.

The backgrounds that tend to fit: consultants from McKinsey, BCG or Bain;
investment banking analysts two to four years in; and people who founded
something that did not work and learned more from it than most people learn
from something that did.

## What we are bad at, stated up front

- We have no financial reporting worth the name.
- Error rates are unmeasured in three departments.
- Almost every internal system runs through one engineer.
- Most of our standard procedures do not exist.

If that reads as a warning, this is not your role.

## How the process runs

Written questions first, before any call. Then a timed simulation of the actual
work. Then an open ended one, where we measure what you choose to do with
unlimited time. Our own hiring data says the interview is the worst predictor we
have and prior work history is the best, so you are judged on what you produce.
