# Business Systems Lead

Own the automations and the customer systems the whole operation runs through.

## Who we are

We are a small trading firm in alternative assets. Around fifty people. A large
part of how the company works day to day is workflow automation and a CRM
holding well over a hundred thousand contacts. It works. It was not designed.

## The job

- Own the automation stack end to end. GoHighLevel, Airtable, Make and the glue
  between them.
- Map the high volume workflows that exist, then build the versions that should
  exist.
- Write the requirements where a real engineering build is needed, and hand
  them over cleanly.
- Kill the manual steps. Most of them are manual because nobody had time, not
  because they have to be.

## You

- You have designed and deployed custom CRM systems and workflow automations
  that other people then relied on daily.
- Advanced in GoHighLevel, Airtable, Make or the equivalent. Name the biggest
  production workflow you have built.
- Strong operations instincts. You have worked somewhere fast moving where the
  process was the product.
- Self directed. There is nobody here to hand you a ticket.

## What we are bad at, stated up front

- We are mid migration from an old CRM to a custom one and both are live.
- Almost none of our standard procedures are written down.
- We have not yet built a good way to assess people for this seat, so the
  founder takes these interviews personally. You will get a direct
  conversation rather than a polished process.
