# Technical Support Specialist

Run the process exactly, catch what other people miss, and say when something
looks wrong.

## Who we are

We are a small trading firm in alternative assets. Around fifty people. This
role is the front line of how the work actually gets done.

## The job

- Follow the playbook exactly, and log what you did.
- Work in software other companies built badly. Being unbothered by that is
  most of the job.
- Notice when a small detail is off, and stop rather than continue.
- Write one clear message about a messy situation, so it can be answered in one
  round trip.
- Handle correspondence with outside support teams: polite, persistent and
  credible.

## You

- Detail driven and completionist. You finish things.
- Technically capable. Not a developer, but nothing on a screen scares you.
- Steady. The work has slow stretches and sudden problems.
- Comfortable with arithmetic, percentages and word problems.
- Based in the region, with reliable transport. There is regular local travel.

New graduates and people early in their career are welcome. Quality assurance,
compliance, lab work, claims processing or customer operations experience all
transfer well.

A genuine plus, and we mean it: achievement in competitive video games or
strategy games. Chess, poker and ranked play all select for the same thing this
job needs.

## What we are bad at, stated up front

- The tooling you will use is a work in progress.
- Standard procedures exist for some of this and not all of it.
- There is a real amount of waiting, and some of the work is repetitive.
