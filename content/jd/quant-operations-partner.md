# Quant Operations Partner

Help a small trading team produce more, make fewer preventable mistakes and
keep important work moving.

## Who we are

We are a small trading firm in alternative assets, with a distributed team of
about fifty people. Our work is high volume, operationally detailed and
dependent on good judgment and consistent follow-through.

## The job

This is a pilot role. The exact mix of work will change as we learn where the
role has the most leverage.

- Rotate through working with individual quants while also supporting a pod or
  the broader team asynchronously.
- Keep administrative and operational follow-ups from stalling.
- Review work for preventable mistakes and help improve the underlying process.
- Maintain clear records so the next person can understand what happened.
- Build good working relationships with clients and teammates.
- Notice when someone is blocked, distracted or missing an important step, and
  intervene constructively.

The goal is not activity for its own sake. We will look for higher output per
working hour, higher output per client, more focused working time, better client
rapport and fewer preventable mistakes.

## You

- Mature, reliable and comfortable holding another person accountable without
  turning every issue into a confrontation.
- Strong at quality assurance, operations and administrative follow-through.
- Clear in writing and good at reducing a messy situation to the next action.
- Comfortable with changing responsibilities in an early-stage operating
  environment.
- Able to provide some regular overlap with Eastern Time. Most support can be
  asynchronous, so a full US schedule is not required.

Useful backgrounds include business operations, project coordination, quality
assurance, client operations, executive assistance and trading or payments
operations. The title matters less than evidence that other people became more
effective because you were there.
